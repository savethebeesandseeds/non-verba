The illustrative Norway administrative coverage in web/src/map-country-data.js
is derived from Kartverket's municipality illustration API. The source is a
coarse display dataset, rather than detailed shoreline or survey geometry.

Derivation

Retain all 357 municipality features in the source response. Their official
municipality codes were compared with the current Kartverket municipality
list at retrieval; all 357 unique codes matched, without missing or extra
municipalities. Convert each Polygon to MultiPolygon coordinates and concatenate
the constituent polygons in source feature/polygon order. This produces 365
polygons, 371 rings (including six holes) and 4,759 coordinates, including
ring-closing coordinates.

Normalize ring winding to the GeoJSON convention: exterior rings are
counterclockwise and interior rings clockwise. Only existing vertex order is
reversed. No polygons, holes or vertices are removed, invented, dissolved,
merged, simplified or buffered. Round values to at most five decimal places,
with midpoints rounded away from zero; the source already uses three decimal
places, so this does not change coordinate values. Coordinates are WGS84
longitude, latitude in decimal degrees (EPSG:4326).

All source coordinates fall within longitude >= 0 and latitude 0 to 72 degrees;
Svalbard, Jan Mayen and Bouvet Island are absent. The aggregate bounds are
[4.091, 57.759, 31.762, 71.385]. The administrative polygons include coastal
waters, covering mainland and coastal island locations without requiring
individual island shoreline polygons. They are a polygon union for display;
internal municipality borders should not be presented as a national coastline.

Limitations

The API explicitly describes these polygons as coarsely simplified and only
intended for an illustration map. Boundaries and coastal waters are generalized
and can differ from detailed geography. This is an administrative display mask,
not a land-only coastline, legal/survey map, exact geofence, entitlement
decision, navigation dataset or claim that Non-verba is deployed at a location.
Define actual deployment support and user permissions separately. Kartverket
does not guarantee accuracy or suitability for a particular use.

This notice covers only the bundled administrative illustration geometry.
It does not grant rights to map tiles, tile services, other basemap assets
or Kartverket's detailed Geovekst-derived cached maps.
