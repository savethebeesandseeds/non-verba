RTKLIB source is not vendored in this repository. The development oracle uses
its eph2pos, geodist, ionmodel and tropmodel functions to generate deliberately
synthetic orbit parameters, signal observations and numerical expected results.
The known Budapest coordinate is an authored mathematical test input, not a
captured person's location. These fixtures establish numerical regression
coverage, not physical-device measurements or independent proof of correctness.
