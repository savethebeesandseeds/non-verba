How the calculation works
One completion, one vote
A vote proposes R × time ÷ 60, with R in the configured currency per hour and time in minutes. Money is rounded upward to the nearest minor currency unit.
Time reduces influence
Remaining weight is max(0, 1 − age ÷ lifetime). A new vote weighs 1; it reaches zero at the selected lifetime.
Enough participation
The vote-derived minimum needs at least — distinct Operators with active votes and at least — effective votes—the sum of their remaining weights. Otherwise, the agreement’s baseline price and time apply.
The collective base
Price and expected time use separate upper weighted medians. At exactly half the weight, the higher value wins. The base price is the greater of the protected baseline and the median price.
Demand adds a premium
Loading the configured demand rule…. Zero requests means no premium. When sensitivity is enabled, positive requests with zero availability use the configured cap. The premium is added to the base price.
The Operator’s minimum
Use the greater of the collective minimum and personal R × expected time ÷ 60. An Operator can quote more.

Participation thresholds and premium limits come from this scenario’s configuration.
