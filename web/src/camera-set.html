Developer trial: one shutter takes the rear photo, then the front photo, and retrieves both together. Sequential photos are taken at different times; verification reports the measured exposure-start gap when their clocks are comparable. Concurrent capture is a separate choice requiring device support. Computers can use an explicit single-camera browser request.

This version includes submitted GPS metadata. It does not collect the separate raw GNSS proof or motion evidence.
