## Four stages of priors research

The owner's 4 October clarification distinguishes:

- **Derivation:** develop and define the catalog.
- **Inclusion:** supply the exact definitions and both parties' declarations in
  the case context.
- **Processing:** study how the resolver uses the Contract, claims, evidence and
  priors together.
- **Evaluation:** test the catalog, inclusion method, processing and their
  interaction, feeding findings back into their design.

Earlier notes called the three fronts derivation, incorporation and evaluation.
Incorporation covered questions now made explicit as inclusion and processing.
This is a research clarification, not a change to signed profiles, legal
authority or the current runtime. The [priors note](DISPUTE_PRIORS_NOTES.md#four-research-stages)
develops the stages and their connection to the wider reasoning system.

## Connection to the current work

The [current implementation](../../requests/DISPUTE_PRIORS.md) still uses five dimensions and
250 points per party. Exploring a different catalog does not change existing
signed profiles; any later implemented revision needs explicit versioning and
consent. Requester and Operator remain roles open to people, organizations,
agents and embodied robots under established signing authority.

DP-2 remains closed as accepted workflow integration. The local model remains a
workflow test component, and its recorded negative findings stand. The companion
remains `ANALYSIS_ONLY`, with financial authority `NONE` and settlement policy
`UNSPECIFIED`.

Use [open decisions](../../requests/DISPUTE_PRIORS_DECISIONS.md) for the current decision
boundary, [settlement handling](../../requests/SETTLEMENT_HANDLING.md) for the existing
independently authorized release path, and the [protocol context](../../requests/CONTEXT.md)

interpretations and authorized consequences distinct when resuming the discussion.
