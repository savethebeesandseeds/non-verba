# Dispute-prior decisions and open settlement policy

Development decision record · 29 September 2026

**DP-2 status: closed and accepted for synthetic workflow integration.** The
owner-scope clarification (local review record, not included in this source release)
preserves all recorded model-quality findings. The current local Qwen model is
retained as a workflow test component. Reliable reasoning and interpretation of
priors into settlement are separate future work, not unfinished acceptance
conditions for DP-2. No further model change, prompt tuning or model-quality
experiment is requested for that milestone.

**Selected for implementation:** two separately authored five-dimension profiles;
250 integer points each; a versioned three-party companion annex; native local
two-pass qualitative analysis; exact review/signing; hash-bound case/attempt
histories with signed participant evidence and challenges; portable replay;
development compute limits. Case and runner envelopes have no author signature;
their recorded execution provenance remains an unverified runner claim.

**Not selected:** any formula converting priors or model text into settlement.
The code retains `ANALYSIS_ONLY`, `UNSPECIFIED` and financial authority `NONE`.
No combination of R+M, O+M or R+O gains authority over the other party through this
extension. The existing action matrix remains the only implemented contractual
effect path, with its exact scopes and required proof.

The [current settlement workflow](SETTLEMENT_HANDLING.md) uses that existing core
path when R and O separately authorize an exact permitted release. It does not
derive a release from points, model text or question dispositions. Those remain
interpretations, distinct from verified facts and contractual authority. The
companion has no automatic bridge from analysis into a core Action.

The [dispute lifecycle specification](DISPUTE_LIFECYCLE.md) defines a proposed
review and negotiation interface around these boundaries. General closure,
reopening, escalation and Non Verba accountability remain unselected. The core
already supports signed dispute-opening events; explicitly connecting the
companion case to opening and notice evidence remains to be specified. Existing
`OPEN`/`UNDER_REVIEW` case records grant no closure or escalation authority.

The aim remains an affordable, explainable settlement procedure, potentially
including a payment amount or bounded remedy under a procedure accepted in
advance. `ANALYSIS_ONLY` is this increment's authority boundary, not a permanent
decision to stop at commentary. Formula, authority, evidence requirements,
challenge procedure and economic fallback require separate design and review.

The [priors research notes](../notes/research/DISPUTE_PRIORS_NOTES.md) distinguish
derivation, inclusion, processing and evaluation. The 4 October clarification
separates supplying the declarations in context from reasoning with that context.
The future dimensions and their number remain open. Honest declaration,
consistency over time and the catalog as a possible commitment remain discussion
aims, with no selected credibility mechanism.

The [dispute resolution notes](../notes/research/DISPUTE_RESOLUTION_NOTES.md)
develop the operational procedure, legal review, evidence access, consequences,
deterrents and accountability. The priors note retains the candidate multimodal
resolver and distinct inspection harness; an external model API is a possibility,
rather than a change to the retained runtime.

The [incident handling and insurance notes](../notes/research/INCIDENT_HANDLING_AND_INSURANCE.md)
retain the provisional material-incident route, external coverage as a possible
initial path and a future insurance institution as a direction to develop.
Materiality criteria, coverage checks, privacy, legal authority and the
institutional form remain open; no insurance dependency or launch jurisdiction
has been selected.

The [research context](../notes/research/README.md) connects these subjects to
this baseline. Priors are not sensor reliability weights. Reorganizing the notes
selects no outcome amounts, deterrents, model or evaluation method, reopens no
DP-2 acceptance condition and confers no financial authority.

An unresolved status is not necessarily economically neutral: after work has
occurred, a Requester retaining money and an Operator retaining only future labor
may bear different unrecovered losses. Research must model that exposure and
which evidence or prior changes a candidate remedy. Compute sponsorship alone
does not resolve it. No payout default is inferred from this concern.

Before a future binding mechanism, explicitly decide and review:

1. Whether the scales mean only relative emphasis or permit interpersonal/cardinal
   comparisons; evaluate the chosen dimensions and definitions themselves.
2. How separately authored profiles could be combined, and how conflicting
   priors are resolved without buying points or guessing consent.
3. Treatment of incomplete evidence, uncertainty, silence and inconclusive analysis;
   no current fallback allocation exists.
4. Permitted compensation and remedies, caps, accrued rights, double counting,
   voluntary repair and any new performance commitment.
5. Who may propose, challenge and adopt a candidate rule; exact scope, signatures,
   effective context, migration and independent verification. Model agreement is
   not an authorization rule.
6. Compute sponsorship, actual capacity and prices, activation, response promises,
   overages, privacy/retention, appeal costs and operational responsibilities.
   The existing service worksheet is not selected by a development counter.

Research fixtures are deliberately synthetic. Candidate-policy manifests live in
the separate `research` module with explicit identity, assumptions and limitations;
evaluation currently returns `POLICY_NOT_IMPLEMENTED`. There is no default solver
or conversion to financial action types. Proposed experiments must stay isolated
and cannot be promoted into accepted policy without a separately reviewed design.

The owner subsequently approved local evaluation of
Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507/Q4_K_M. That evaluation is complete; its exact weights remain
the local workflow test component, without acceptance of model-quality claims.
DP-1's SmolLM2-135M Q4_K_M CPU
probe reached the output cap and produced no usable two-pass analysis. Its failed
records remain unchanged. The separately authorized GPU cutover is environment
work; DP-2's retained synthetic experiments have their own specifications and
execution/quality record (local review record, not included in this source release).
Model quality, citation accuracy, role-order sensitivity, verbosity/prestige
effects, injection resistance, costs and allocation sensitivity remain research
questions. No test establishes fairness or strategy resistance.

The initial consent path permits one context per exact Contract. A provisioned
runtime/specification change needs an explicitly supported new context through
an authorized Contract revision or new Assignment; there is no same-context
replacement or general annex-amendment protocol. Designing a narrower separately
endorsed annex succession path is a possible later extension, not current behavior.

The financial core and accepted frozen records remain the reference. DP-2's
workflow integration is complete. Reliable reasoning and an authorized procedure
for interpreting priors into settlement remain unimplemented future work; neither
is an implicit promise hidden in the prompt or a blocker to this closure.
