### D4 retention ceiling

The maximum is two years. Each class has its own start. The end is two years
after that start. Nothing in this rule extends a clock because validation has
not started, because nobody has read the submission, or because a pilot ends.

| Record | Clock starts | Clock ends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Document photo, face photo and typed identity details that have a confirmed storage receipt | That receipt | Two years after that receipt |
| Rejected submission, including its photos and typed details | The stored rejection record | Two years after that record |
| Attempt with no confirmed storage receipt | No service copy enters this rule | A local draft is not a retained intake record |
| Exact notice text and the acknowledgment of that text | The stored acknowledgment | Two years after that acknowledgment |
| Intake receipt and audit records | The receipt | Two years after that receipt |

Two years is a ceiling. The rule does not promise to keep the material for the
whole period. An earlier deletion can apply only when a future D5 process
actually carries it out. D5 is not that process today.

If validation never starts, these clocks still end on time. Inactivity is not
a review and is not a reason to keep the material longer.

If a future pilot ends, new real submissions stop. Material already received
keeps the end date from its own start. Ending the pilot does not open another
two-year period.

Backup copies are D6 and remain unresolved. This ceiling does not permit a
backup to stand in as the live identity record after the primary period ends,
and it does not claim that any backup can currently be expired.

This rule uses no “verified” or “real” claim. A stored receipt confirms
storage, not identity. The rule does not open real-user intake, change Account
Terms, or change synthetic notice v1, v2 or v3.

### Identity Intake Notice

**Purpose and responsible organization.** [D1 legal name and contact] receives
your information for [D2 concrete present purpose] in [D7 selected pilot scope].
The applicable processing basis is [D2 reviewed basis and any additional
conditions]. Document ingestion and validation are still being developed. This
intake does not currently verify your identity or give work, signing or sensor
permission. No review start or completion time is confirmed.

**What you submit.** We receive [D2 final required/optional fields and images],
plus the account binding, operation/content identifiers, exact notice
acknowledgment and receipt/status records needed for [D2 defined purposes].
[D2 explain what is optional and what happens if required information is not
provided]. The photos are re-encoded as bounded JPEGs before transfer. This does
not anonymize what is visible in them. This intake does not include automatic
text extraction, face recognition, biometric feature extraction, liveness,
training or public/task-evidence reuse.

**Where the information goes and who can access it.** The information is held
in [D1 actual storage and transfer description]. Access is limited to your
authenticated account and [D3 authorized roles/recipients and purposes].
[D3 accurately state whether any review process exists and who is responsible].
It is not a public profile or another participant's ordinary task evidence.

**How long it is kept.** Photos and entered details that have a confirmed
storage receipt are kept for at most two years after that receipt. A rejected
submission is kept for at most two years after its stored rejection record.
An attempt with no confirmed storage receipt is not a retained service copy.
The exact notice text, its acknowledgment, and the intake receipt and audit
records are kept for at most two years after the record that stores them.
These periods are maximums. They do not run until review. If validation never
starts, the same clocks still end. If a pilot ends, material already received
keeps its original end date. Backups and restore remain [D6 actual rules, delay
and restore treatment].

**Correction, deletion and your rights.** Contact [D5/D7 working route] for
[D5/D7 applicable correction, deletion, access and other rights/process].
[D5 explain scope, exceptions, outcomes and account-closure effects]. Leaving
the page or signing out removes neither an already submitted service copy nor
its backup copies. [D7 applicable complaint route and, if consent is relied on,
the distinct purpose-specific withdrawal process and effects].

**Your submission choice.** Review the selected information and this complete
notice before the separate Submit action. Opening the document or acknowledging
it does not upload the package. A changed package or notice requires fresh
review. A received storage receipt confirms storage, not identity approval. If
the reply is interrupted, check the original operation; do not assume nothing
was saved or submit a new package automatically.

The end-of-document control can acknowledge the notice and return to review;
it is not a substitute for choosing a valid processing basis. If that basis
requires a distinct consent request, its exact purpose, choice and withdrawal
behavior must be resolved in D2/D7 and implemented before collection. Do not
silently reuse the synthetic-v1 agreement for this new scope.

## Status wording and admission

After a **confirmed receipt only**, the proposed compact status is:

> Submission received. Your identity is not verified. Document ingestion and
> validation are still being developed. No review start or completion time is
> confirmed.

Before a receipt, preserve the unavailable/failed/unknown state actually
reported by the service. “Processing takes time” alone would imply a running
process without identifying one. Any later queue, reviewer or estimate must
reflect actual capability and receive its own scope review.

If a future real-user scope is requested and D1-D7 are decided, Hx02 prepares
the final successor text and exact digest;
Hx10 and the private service owner independently pin its bytes, implement the
selected lifecycle/access and authorization behavior, and evidence it before
runtime admission for that scope. Approval of this worksheet alone neither
enables real-data intake nor supplies implementation proof. This does not
replace the current test-data-only direction. Keep all synthetic v1 text,
catalog rows and historical acceptances immutable, and retain the original
Account Terms. A new purpose or verification pipeline does not silently reuse
an earlier acknowledgment.

## Conditional legal review inputs

Reviewed 23 October 2026. Coverage is undecided; these EU sources are review
inputs only if applicable, not a selection of EU or global coverage. The Irish
Data Protection Commission's [Article 13/14 transparency guidance](https://www.dataprotection.ie/en/individuals/know-your-rights/right-be-informed-transparency-article-13-14-gdpr)
identifies controller/contact, purpose/basis, recipients/transfers, retention
period or criteria, rights and consequences of non-provision among notice
inputs. This supports the decision-sheet structure, not this proposal's legal
approval. The EDPB's [Guidelines 3/2019, section 5.1](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sites/default/files/files/file1/edpb_guidelines_201903_video_devices_en_0.pdf)
distinguish ordinary image capture from specific technical processing for unique
identification. Applicable review must assess actual processing; neither all
photos nor the absence of face features alone settles every legal requirement.
No processing basis, statutory retention period or identity-assurance standard
is selected here.
