The requirements below define the already authorized synthetic-only development
exercise. The dated technical checkpoints in the evidence section now record
the scoped integration and synthetic checks; Hx02's original module checks
establish the document contract separately. The linked checkpoint records the
unavailable gate before the owner's later test-only activation direction below.
A synthetic-only notice is not an automatic classifier capable of
preventing a user from selecting a real photo, and the technical checks do not
authorize real-data storage or establish legal adoption.

- Present the complete pinned text, title, synthetic/draft scope and version
  before a fresh deliberate acknowledgment, using the accessible document
  process already used for Account Terms. Opening, scrolling, Back, account
  signup agreement or camera permission alone must not accept this notice or
  submit media. Keep the synthetic-only restriction visible before selection
  and submission; do not call this a production consent or adopted Privacy Notice.
- Bind acknowledgment and final submission to the exact fields, processed JPEGs,
  operation/review revision, notice id/version/digest and current authenticated
  account/principal/session/recovery context. Any changed input or notice requires
  fresh review. Processing permission and final storage acknowledgment stay
  distinct. No upload happens merely by choosing photos or opening the document.
- The service independently pins the approved development catalog's full text,
  format and exact digest, exposes the current supported document, refuses
  mismatches or unavailable policy, and retains immutable text and attributable
  acknowledgment with the accepted submission. Client-provided hash/text is not
  itself authority. Record service-observed time/provenance rather than treating
  a client clock as proof.
- Both image sources must actually pass the selected bounded JPEG normalization
  before transfer. Do not describe original selected bytes as normalized or infer
  anonymity from metadata removal. Scope the body to the five declared fields,
  two selected photos and necessary binding/receipt metadata. Never put raw
  images, identity details, credentials or recovery factors in status/log output.
- Enforce submitting-account access through the API and explicitly authorized
  development administration. No public object URLs, discovery projection,
  cross-account access or ordinary task-evidence reuse. Specify administrative
  access and storage/key protection in the private evidence, not by assuming that
  authentication alone supplies custody protection.
- Exact retries preserve the original text, acknowledgment and receipt; changed
  bytes conflict. An uncertain/late response requires original-operation lookup.
  Cancellation, sign-out, local cleanup and test-account tombstoning cannot
  establish service deletion. Retrieval must distinguish a historical receipt
  from current retained status and future deletion outcomes.
- Keep custody receipt separate from the synthetic review outcome and genuine
  identity status. Neither a storage receipt nor a test-review decision supplies

  sensors. V3 authorizes only the bounded synthetic review exercise; no OCR,

## Custody and unresolved decisions

For this exercise, “custody” identifies the selected private development service,
not a newly selected legal controller, reviewer or production operator. Durable
synthetic storage outlives the page. The draft deliberately selects **no
retention duration or automatic expiry** and promises no implemented erasure
route. Synthetic test retention still needs an explicit operational lifecycle;
it is not a precedent for retaining real identity material.

| Decision | Required resolution before real-data use or public adoption |
| --- | --- |
| Responsible organization and purpose | Name the legal controller/custodian, service/contact/rights route, actual purpose and applicable legal requirements; distinguish development administration from identity-review authority. |
| Necessary data and eligible users | Justify each field and document/photo requirement, age versus birth-date handling and freshness, permitted document types, role/level policy, eligible ages/capacity and representation. A numeric form range is not an eligibility decision. |
| Storage and access | Identify actual stores, authorized staff/reviewers, access/audit process, encryption/key control, processors/recipients and transfers. Verify account isolation and avoid task/public reuse. |
| Retention | Select a justified duration or determination criteria separately for photos, entered details, rejected/abandoned submissions, acceptance text and audit/receipt records. Specify start/end triggers; no period is silently adopted here. |
| Deletion and closure | Define the supported request/operation, authority, media-versus-metadata scope, pending/failed/confirmed outcomes, exceptions and response process. Distinguish test-account closure from intake deletion and rights handling. |
| Backups and recovery | Define backup/version retention, expiry or deletion treatment, restore suppression/reapplication, restored access and proof of the affected copies. Do not promise immediate purge or let an old receipt recreate deleted material. |
| Review and adoption | Select the decision maker, process, criteria, correction/appeal route and assurance meaning before any identity award. Complete applicable legal review, languages, notice delivery and authorized adoption separately from draft acknowledgment. |

The [Account Terms review](ACCOUNT_TERMS_REVIEW.md#review-findings-and-adoption-decisions)
and [legal register](../LEGAL_DOCUMENT_REGISTER.md) retain the broader unresolved
operator, countries, privacy and adoption decisions. This record makes no new
