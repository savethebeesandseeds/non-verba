# Representation audit rubric — PROTOTYPE

Record an actual case reading before judging adequacy. Read the complete referenced
parent, its surrounding normalized text where necessary, and the corresponding
original source passage. An excerpt or successful hash check does not replace that
reading. Preserve the exact audit ID, packet hash, source version, reviewer exposure,
reading scope and reason for every decision. Unread cases remain unread.
The legacy `complete_parent` sampling category refers to the full stored flat
span. Exact range coverage does not verify its governing hierarchy or semantic
completeness. Record detached governing text, introduced lists and associated
qualifications as boundary failures when they prevent reconstructing the provision.

`context_adequate` means the retained parent and accessible surrounding source let
the reviewer reconstruct the sampled provision's actors, operative rule, conditions,
exceptions, negations, amounts and continuations. A correct string offset alone is
insufficient. An unexplained boundary that separates a material qualification is
inadequate even when the separated text survives elsewhere in the document.

`heading_adequate` means the displayed hierarchy corresponds to the available
source structure and does not promote an address, page label or body sentence into
a misleading title. When the source genuinely has no recoverable title, an explicit
unknown/unverified hierarchy can be adequate for representation if the reviewer
can locate the provision and no invented structure is presented as verified. This
judgment does not establish legal structure or a normative meaning.

`material_loss_observed` records a missing or misleading condition, exception,
negation, amount, actor or continuation encountered in that reading.
`unresolved_material_loss` remains true until a retained repaired source version
has been re-read. Repairs preserve earlier failures, use a new source identity and
need a fresh audit. CJK/script warnings and model-transfer limitations remain
visible; source preservation does not validate an English-trained encoder for
every language.

Development can stop on a concrete material failure, with the reviewed denominator
and unread cases reported honestly. A passing final development record requires
all 200 cases to have been read. The fresh check uses 100 different, purpose-reserved
cases and requires at least 95 with adequate context and headings, plus zero
unresolved observed material losses. Report strata and uncertainty alongside that
rate. These are preparatory agent engineering judgments, not participant findings,
independent human review, legal advice or a guarantee about every source provision.

The sealing utility validates references and coverage of authored judgments. It
cannot independently attest that a reviewer read a source or prove those judgments
correct. Coding packets omit inferred function labels, clusters and supplied prior
names. Two separate open codings and reconciliation remain required before meanings
are frozen; this rubric does not choose candidate names or their number.

After an observed source failure, a repair audit may predeclare a supplementary
protocol before drawing another packet. Keep the original purpose assignment;
exclude every declared previously exposed contract from the fresh pool. Sample
100 sections from accepted reconstructable sources, and report their distinct
contract count and repeated-section correlation. Audit **every rejected source**
separately as an integrity control. Correctly excluding an image-only filing does
not count as reconstructing its missing body or as passing fresh adequacy. The
failed packet and judgments remain retained. Other unknown exposure stays unknown.
Development and negative pools continue to include rejected-source anchors.

Material source findings from any pool hold expansion, including findings made
after a gate was sealed. The immutable observed-issue record binds the actual
packet, source and reading evidence. A new extraction identity alone does not
clear it: preserve the original finding and bind a separate actual rereading of
the repaired parent and original context to that exact source version. Literal
inclusion and read-range checks support this record; they do not supply a judgment
or independently attest that reading occurred.
