# Priors Derivation Lab v0.2 — PROTOTYPE

This isolated research attempt executes the [expanded study protocol](../plans/STUDY_0_2_PLAN.md).
Its outputs have **authority NONE**, and no candidate is adopted. The completed
v0.1 recipe and retained results remain the baseline. The source lives entirely
in this subtree; generated corpus material stays in the existing managed Debian
volume under `/opt/nonverba-build/priors-derivation/study-v0.2/<run-name>/`.

The study freezes the complete pinned MCC metadata census and 15,000 selections:
6,000 broad discovery, 4,000 services-label enrichment, 3,000 confirmation and
2,000 later-period replication. Filer assignments are disjoint, with at most two
records per assigned filer. Services labels are machine-generated proxies for
assignment relevance. Failed downloads remain failures, without replacement.
The first 1,000 discovery records are a calibration checkpoint. Confirmation
and later-period material stay sealed until candidate wording and evaluation
rules have been frozen.

Full normalized documents, reconstructed parent spans, every extracted unit and
exclusions remain inspectable. At most 40 units per contract enter provisional
selection, followed by contract-balanced admission. The shared 300,000-unit main
budget reserves 200,000 for discovery, 60,000 for confirmation and 40,000 for
temporal evaluation; unused capacity is not silently reassigned.
Conservative repetition families retain meaningful variants and separate broader
template hypotheses. Parent offsets identify UTF-16 positions in normalized
text, rather than byte positions in the original HTML.
Reading every character of a stored parent establishes coverage of that extracted
span. Its legal or semantic completeness remains unverified until the relevant
original source is read; a flat range is not proof of a complete provision.

The pinned MiniLM-L6 baseline uses real bounded CPU batches, measured tokenizer
windows and retained float32 shards. Discovery uses a contract/family-balanced
20,000-unit reservoir and all 20 declared k/repeat settings. The primary setting
is k=64, repeat=1. The [comparison recipe](comparison-recipe.json) separately pins
MiniLM-L12 and a 30,000-unit comparison target, alternative section context and
a lexical baseline. Counts, timing and outcomes must come from retained run
manifests; configuration alone does not establish execution.

Representation auditing uses full source material, including material excluded
from embedding: 200 development checks, 100 fresh checks, 500 unprimed coding
sections and 200 difficult/negative cases. Packet construction has status
`AUDIT_NOT_RUN`. Actual review records and fresh adequacy determine whether
processing can expand beyond calibration. Agent coding and desk judgments are
preparatory evidence; participant comprehension and external-source transfer
remain separate phases.

## Running the study

Follow the [container plan](../../../../../docs/development/CONTAINER_PLAN.md) and
[snapshot guide](../../../../../docs/development/CONTAINER_MIGRATION.md). The wrapper
uses the existing verified container and pinned dependencies, installing no
Windows toolchain and making no container lifecycle changes. From the public
checkout:

```powershell
$study = './code/disputes/prototypes/priors-derivation/study-v0.2/Invoke-Study.ps1'
& $study -Action Test -ModelSmoke
& $study -Action Init -RunName new-study-name
& $study -Action Acquire -RunName new-study-name -Limit 1000
& $study -Action Extract -RunName new-study-name -Limit 1000
& $study -Action Audit -RunName new-study-name -Manifest processing/SOURCE/calibration-1000/extraction-manifest.json
& $study -Action Prepare -RunName new-study-name -Manifest processing/SOURCE/calibration-1000/extraction-manifest.json
& $study -Action Embed -RunName new-study-name -Manifest admission/STAGE/manifest.json
& $study -Action Discover -RunName new-study-name -Manifest embeddings/STAGE/manifest.json
& $study -Action Verify -RunName new-study-name -Embeddings embeddings/STAGE/manifest.json -Geometry geometry/STAGE/manifest.json
```

Replace `SOURCE` and `STAGE` with the exact manifest paths printed by the preceding
command. Init freezes a new run. Completed stages are immutable; resumes verify
retained bytes and identities. An interrupted partial init needs a new named run,
while acquisition and processing checkpoints resume the same frozen selection.
The whole-study source snapshot and individual stage source hashes are recorded
when available. An absent snapshot environment value remains absent.

After the calibration gate passes, acquire the same discovery prefix with
`-Limit 10000` and extract with `-FullDiscovery -Limit 10000`. Preserve cumulative
1,000/3,000/6,000/10,000 checkpoints. The currently executing run is
`20261013-study-15000`; inspect its actual progress before resuming a worker.
One acquisition writer is enforced, and a timeout does not prove that worker
has stopped. Respect recorded throttling pauses rather than restarting around them.

The read-only paginated review uses the existing container port 4173 and its
documented loopback mapping. Preserve another service if the port is occupied.
Use the exact admission and geometry manifest paths with `-Action Serve`.
The local review entry is `noindex`, keeps scripts strict, and permits only the
selector's narrow style-element exception. Corpus payload is fetched in bounded
pages instead of importing all clauses or vectors into a browser.

Resource ceilings are 4 MiB per raw contract, 8 GiB raw bytes, 24 GiB total run
storage and a 6 GiB processing-memory target. Exhaustion pauses work while
preserving existing objects. No ceiling increase or data deletion is automatic.
